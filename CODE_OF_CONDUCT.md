# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NeverMore to be a friendly, safe and welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion, or beliefs. We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful to others
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Assuming good intent, and asking questions when something is unclear
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic demands
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and other project channels) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@Someshog](https://github.com/Someshog), through GitHub (for sensitive matters, use a private channel such as a [security advisory](https://github.com/Someshog/NeverMore/security) or the contact method on their profile). All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

Maintainers may respond with a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.
